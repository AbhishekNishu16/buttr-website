# Buttr knowledge

The facts the website and its AI may use. If something is not here, the AI says it does not know and offers a wealth partner. Keep this file up to date; it is the single source for AI answers.

## Mission and philosophy

* Mission: Wealthelp. Making financial guidance free, simple and always available.
* Like a family doctor, for your financial wellbeing.
* Philosophy: You’ve worked hard for your money. Getting the right help with it shouldn’t be hard, too.
* People first, Buttr always present. A service, not an app.

## Who it is for

* Families with roughly ₹5 Cr to ₹30 Cr of wealth in India's largest metros: Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi and similar.

## The problem

* About 89% of India's investable wealth has no professional guidance (about 11% is professionally managed, against 68% in the US and 29% in China).
* Fewer than 1,000 registered advisers for 1.4 billion people.
* Good advice has been priced for the very rich, so most people figure it out alone.

## How Buttr works

1. Understand you. A 45 minute conversation with a wealth partner, by video, phone or in person: your goals, your worries, your family. No products, no pitch.
2. Connect finances. With consent, Account Aggregator brings banks, funds, stocks, EPF and insurance together in about 15 seconds. Read only.
3. Build your Blueprint. Your Financial Wellbeing Score, gaps, risks and opportunity cost, and where your next rupee should go.
4. Next steps with your partner. Every number shown after tax, risk shown beside every return, nothing moves without your approval.
5. Live with Buttr. Buttr in your pocket between meetings: answers any hour, actions lined up by your partner, check ins when life changes.

## What Buttr covers

* A 360 degree approach, from financial planning to tax optimisation.
* Each customer works closely with a human finance expert as their wealth partner, and Buttr AI as their finance concierge, monitoring things 24/7, all through the Buttr app.
* Investments, insurance, loans and tax together. Every recommendation after tax.
* Calm in market falls: within hours, a message linking the fall to your plan. Permission to spend when you're ahead.
* Financial Wellbeing Score: one number out of 100, with a starting score and a target.

## The Blueprint

The plan a customer leaves the first meeting with: what we found (money being lost each year, by source), the plan in three moves with timing, and the detail behind each move, which can be opened.

## Pocket Buttr

## Wealth partners

## Cost and how Buttr earns

* No fees for customers. Nothing upfront.
* Buttr is a mutual fund distributor registered with AMFI and regulated by SEBI, working within suitability guidelines.
* Revenue is commission paid by product providers, for example an equity fund trail of roughly 0.5% to 0.9% a year, paid from the fund's expense ratio.
* Invest ₹10,000 and Buttr earns about ₹80 that year. A Commission Receipt shows exactly what Buttr earns.
* Recommendations are made blind to commission rates, and every recommendation shows Buttr's commission beside alternatives.
* Buttr never executes transactions on its own. Customers complete every action themselves.
